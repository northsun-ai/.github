# Code of Conduct

We want Northsun's projects to be welcoming and productive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, even when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery
- Personal or political attacks and trolling
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.

Report concerns to **abdur@northsun.ai**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
